# Visual review: revision 5.1

## Findings from revision 5.0

The original paper identity was retained, but the page read as a specification: long paragraphs, a repetitive section hierarchy and a large control panel whose selected state repeated routine-first positioning. On mobile the trace became a long stack of technical details. These are concrete problems in this output, not evidence about a model's training or an inherent ranking of models.

No controlled comparison with an Opus 5.5 output was conducted. Its prompt, exact harness and comparable artifact were not available. Do not claim the cause of a performance gap is known.

## Research used

Anthropic's frontend-design guidance advocates choosing a specific visual concept, treating typography and copy as design material, exploring before implementation and critiquing rendered output. Its own Opus 5.5 prompting guide warns that generic-looking frontend defaults also occur in Opus. These sources support a better workflow; they do not prove a head-to-head win.

## Decisions

Keep the actual blue-pen/engineering-paper identity. Make the employee the centre of a non-linear capability map. Give the evidence example a dossier composition with a source inspector instead of six stacked screens. Give governance a dark architectural plate with explicit permission boundary. Use an editorial ledger for product choices; reserve numbering for the three stages of enablement.

Keep markup, CSS and progressive enhancement separate. Use real semantic controls and readable DOM text; SVG supplies the connective geometry. Preserve Layout.astro and its existing desk components. The new stylesheet namespaces content classes to avoid collisions with earlier components.

## What the graphics mean

The capability map is conceptual, not a quantitative chart. The evidence bars count 12 explicitly fictional records (8 remote, 3 audit, 1 training); they are not business performance claims. The infrastructure diagram is an implementation principle, not a claim that bb-app ships these corporate controls.

## Next comparison

A meaningful Opus comparison would use the same repository snapshot, brief, fonts, assets, tools and effort budget. Evaluate blind on identity, information hierarchy, conceptual accuracy, responsive behaviour and interaction quality. This comparison has not been run and no numeric score is asserted.
