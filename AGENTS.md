# ROM4 working rules

## Positioning

Sell employees' ability to investigate, model, create and verify with AI, supported by company governance. Do not regress to a routine-discovery funnel, mandatory single-task pilot, CRM/RPA sales language or time-savings headline. Existing tools first; custom work only for demonstrated gaps. No fabricated cases, results, compliance, zero lock-in or hidden chain-of-thought promises.

## Visual identity

The approved reference is ROM4's editorial engineering sheet: off-white paper, fine grid, dark green-black ink, blue pen accents, serif display, sans reading text, monospace captions. Keep existing desk/layout machinery unless explicitly asked to change it. Default fonts and cream palettes are not forbidden here: the user's actual identity overrides generic anti-template advice.

Design information, not only containers. Each major section needs its own appropriate composition: annotated sheet, inspectable trace, architecture plate, comparative ledger. Avoid repeated card grids, pills, generic dashboards, decorative metrics, purple gradients, gratuitous glass or cybersecurity imagery. One human handwritten annotation is enough. Numbers denote actual order, not decoration.

## Verify before claiming

Inspect desktop and mobile screenshots, including the middle of the page, dark mode and the smallest supported width. Test keyboard, no-JavaScript and reduced-motion states. Clearly label demos and their actual computation/storage. A screenshot of standalone HTML is not proof the complete Astro site built. A queued CI job is not a passed job. State unavailable tests.

Consult docs/design-review.md and docs/positioning.md before changing the page. Preserve source/version/action distinctions and data permissions when evolving demos.
